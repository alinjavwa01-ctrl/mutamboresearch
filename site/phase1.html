The first beef billion

Before $10B there is $1B — the government's own target. This is the costed first stage: six hubs on the existing spine, ~$3 billion of capital, and a five-year run to a billion dollars of exports.

Target  $1B/yr exports by 2031 Capital  ≈ $3.0B Where  Southern Province heartland
01

The shape of a billion

A billion dollars of beef is roughly 167,000 tonnes of product a year — about 930,000 head slaughtered for export. From today's near-zero base, that is the entire job of Phase 1.

It does not require the national herd transformation that $10B does. It requires concentrating on the herd Zambia already has in Southern Province, lifting off-take from ~8% toward ~15%, finishing cattle to heavier weights, and standing up the first export-grade abattoirs, cold chain and disease compartment. Everything is within reach of the existing rail spine.

02

Six hubs on the spine

Every Phase-1 node already sits on, or one feeder from, the Zambia Railways spine through the cattle belt.

Kalomo — where the first DRC consignment was slaughtered — is upgraded to export grade first. Choma–Monze, in the heart of the herd, becomes the new flagship. Namwala feeds finishing cattle in; Lusaka adds air-freight for premium cuts; Kapiri Mposhi is the reefer pivot onto TAZARA; Ndola serves the Copperbelt and the Lobito interchange.

03

The $3 billion, itemised

The biggest Phase-1 line isn't steel — it's animals: the finance, genetics and finishing that turn a low-off-take herd into export supply.

05

How it's financed

No single lender funds a $3B agro-industrial build. Phase 1 stacks capital by risk appetite.

The herd line ($1.0B)

Off-take-backed working capital, warehouse/livestock receipts, and contract-farming (out-grower) finance — repaid from the cattle themselves. This is where smallholders enter the value chain as suppliers, not spectators.

The hard assets ($1.8B)

DFI debt (IFC, AfDB, DFC via Lobito, DBSA) and PPP concessions for abattoirs, cold hubs, reefer and power, crowded in by a sovereign guarantee and anchored by off-take agreements with regional and Gulf buyers.

The gate comes first The $0.35B disease-compartment & LITS line is small but sequenced first: without it, Phase 1 is capped at low-value regional trade and never reaches the Gulf or EU rungs that make $1B possible. See The Disease-Free Gate.
